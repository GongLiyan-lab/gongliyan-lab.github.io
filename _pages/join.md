# Join Our Lab

We are looking for highly motivated researchers to join our lab focusing on stem‑cell‑derived organoid, vascular biology and tumor microenvironment.

### Open Positions
**Associate Research Scientist（副研究员） / Assistant Research Scientist（助理研究员） / Postdoctoral Fellow（博士后）**

### Job Responsibilities
1. Independently conduct research under the supervision of PI, including project design, experimental implementation and data analysis.
2. Assist in mentoring graduate students and research staff; participate in project management and daily lab organization.
3. Contribute to lab research development and project execution; participate in manuscript preparation and grant application.

### Requirements
1. PhD obtained or expected in biochemistry, molecular biology, cell biology, stem cell & regenerative medicine, oncology, immunology, bioinformatics or related disciplines.
2. Strong passion for scientific research, capability of independent thinking and problem‑solving, high sense of responsibility and strict scientific integrity.
3. Good communication and teamwork skills; willing to mentor students and collaborate within the group.
4. Solid capability for reading academic literature and scientific writing; first‑author publications published or expected in recent two years.
5. Candidates with experience in one or more of the following areas will be prioritized:
    - Tumor organoids, vascular organoids, stem‑cell differentiation and multicellular interaction models
    - Tumor microenvironment, immunotherapy mechanisms and immune‑cell functional studies
    - Single‑cell sequencing, spatial transcriptomics, WES, RNA‑seq and multi‑omics data analysis
    - NGS library construction, probe capture, sequencing and bioinformatic pipelines

### How to Apply
Please send your CV together with a brief research statement to our lab email for further inquiry.
